Day to day that means PowerMTA, KumoMTA, Postfix, Haraka, Momentum and GreenArrow in production; queueing, throttling and retry behaviour; DNS and the SPF, DKIM, DMARC, MTA-STS and TLS-RPT stack; IP pool design and warm-up; bounce, deferral and complaint classification; and blocklist remediation when reputation goes wrong. I have run outbound estates on bare metal and cloud across several hosting providers, and led the team that operated them.
